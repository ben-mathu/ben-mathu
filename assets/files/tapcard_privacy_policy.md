# TapCard Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: September 19, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how **Benatt** ("we," "our," or "I"), operated by Benard Mathu, collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use the **TapCard** mobile application (the "Service").

## 1. Information Collection and Use

TapCard is designed to allow you to create, manage, and share digital business card information. To provide these features, the application processes information that you choose to enter or share through the Service.

- **Personal and Contact Information:** You may choose to provide information such as your name, job title, company, phone number, email address, website, physical address, social media profiles, and other information included in your digital business card.

- **vCard Information:** TapCard may create, import, export, or share contact information using the vCard format. Information contained in a vCard is processed according to your actions and may be stored locally on your device or shared with another person or application when you choose to do so.

- **NFC:** TapCard may use your device's Near Field Communication (NFC) capabilities to read or write compatible NFC tags and facilitate the sharing of digital business card information. NFC functionality is activated only when you choose to use an NFC-related feature. TapCard does not continuously monitor or track nearby NFC devices.

- **QR Codes:** TapCard may generate QR codes containing information from your digital business card. When you choose to display or share a QR code, the information encoded in that QR code may be read by another person or compatible application.

- **Sharing:** TapCard may use Android's sharing functionality to allow you to share your digital business card, vCard files, QR codes, links, or other supported content with applications or people of your choice. Once information is shared with another person or third-party application, its subsequent use is governed by the recipient's or third party's own privacy practices.

- **On-Device Data:** Information created or entered into TapCard is stored locally on your device. TapCard does not transmit or store your digital business card information on our servers or in cloud storage.

## 2. Third-Party Services

TapCard may use third-party services required to provide specific application functionality, such as Android system services and other services necessary for features such as sharing or NFC functionality.

TapCard does not use Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics for analytics or crash reporting.

TapCard does not use third-party cloud storage to remotely store your digital business card information.

## 3. Log Data and Cookies

TapCard does not explicitly use cookies.

If an error occurs, technical information may be processed by the application or a third-party service used to provide a particular feature. Such information may include technical details such as device type, operating system version, application version, and error information where necessary to diagnose or resolve technical problems.

TapCard does not intentionally collect information from your device that is unrelated to providing the functionality of the Service.

## 4. Service Providers

TapCard does not transmit your digital business card information to service providers for remote storage or processing.

Third-party applications and services may receive information when you explicitly choose to share your digital business card, vCard, QR code, or other supported content with them. Their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

## 5. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

TapCard does not sell your personal information.

Information contained in your digital business card is shared with other people or applications only when you explicitly choose to use features such as NFC, QR codes, vCard export, or Android's sharing functionality.

TapCard does not automatically transmit your digital business card information to us or to a remote server.

We may disclose information where necessary to comply with applicable law, legal processes, or valid governmental requests. However, because TapCard does not remotely store your digital business card information, we generally do not possess that locally stored information.

## 6. Data Storage and Security

TapCard stores digital business card information locally on your device.

The security of locally stored information therefore depends partly on the security of your device, including its operating-system security, screen lock, encryption, and other security mechanisms provided by the device.

I use reasonable measures within the application to protect information stored by TapCard. However, no method of electronic storage is completely secure, and TapCard cannot guarantee the absolute security of information stored on your device.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

TapCard does not remotely retain your digital business card information.

Information created or stored by TapCard remains on your device until you delete it through the application, delete the application's stored data, or uninstall the application.

When you share information through NFC, QR codes, vCards, or another sharing mechanism, the recipient may retain or copy that information. TapCard cannot control or delete information after it has been shared with another person or third-party application.

## 8. Children's Privacy

TapCard is not directed toward children under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because TapCard stores digital business card information locally on the user's device and does not transmit that information to our servers, we generally have no ability to identify, access, or remotely delete information stored by the application on a user's device.

If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information to us directly, for example by contacting us through our support email, they may contact us using the details below so that we can address the request.

Parents and guardians can also remove locally stored TapCard information from the device by deleting the relevant information within the application or uninstalling the application.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically.

Any changes will be reflected by posting the updated Privacy Policy and changing the **Last Updated** date at the beginning of this policy.

You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
